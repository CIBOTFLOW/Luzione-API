   database; repair runtime schema errors before activating new effects.
2. Add API-owned global identity/membership, connections, authority v2,
   commands, approvals, durable workflows, provider receipts, audit, usage,
   budgets and kill switches through additive migrations.

   honest health and history in the UI Connection Center.

   model prices, usage receipts and reviewed learning candidates.
5. Prove the allowlisted internal-mailbox golden workflow, including exact
   approval, one send, reply capture, proposal, outcome and recovery. Disable
   the pilot send flag after evidence capture.
6. Prove tenant onboarding, security, backup/restore, load, observability,
   preview promotion and rollback before a pilot-ready claim.

Authority v2 is: A0 read/analyze/simulate; A1 internal drafts/recommendations/
tasks; A2 bounded reversible external effects; A3 binding/high-impact effects;
A4 prohibited. Historical receipts keep their original contract version.

Pilot readiness scores golden journey, tenancy, governance, integrations,
reliability, AI cost, security, recovery, observability and operations equally.
It requires at least 85/100, no zero category, and hard passes for tenant
isolation, A4 denial, restore, schema parity, rollback and the controlled canary.
